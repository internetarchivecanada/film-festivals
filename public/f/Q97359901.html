Ottawa Canadian Film Festival

Also: OCan · OCanFilmFest · Festival de films canadiens d’Ottawa

OCan is a Film Festival in Ottawa that celebrates Canadian film with annual festival screenings in November, usually held at the ByTowne Cinema.
